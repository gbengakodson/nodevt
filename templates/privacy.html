Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

1. Introduction

NODE ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by THRIVALBASE LTD (RC: 1394827). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform (nodevt.com) and mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and encrypted password.
  • KYC Data: Government-issued ID type, ID number, nationality, and country (when you verify your identity).
  • Blockchain Data: Your unique BSC wallet address (publicly visible on the blockchain).
  • Transaction Data: Deposit amounts, withdrawal addresses, and trade history.
  • Device Data: Browser type, IP address, and device identifiers for security and analytics.
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our support chat and community feed.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process deposits, withdrawals, and automated trading operations.
  • To comply with legal obligations (KYC/AML verification).
  • To send transactional emails (deposit confirmations, withdrawal OTPs, daily portfolio summaries).
  • To improve our platform and provide customer support.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • Blockchain Transactions: Your wallet address and transaction hashes are publicly visible on the BSC network.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties that help us operate (e.g., email delivery, cloud hosting). They are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including AES-256 encryption for private keys, SSL/TLS for data transmission, and secure cloud infrastructure. However, no method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Certain transactional records may be retained for legal compliance.

7. Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data through your profile settings.
  • Export your transaction history.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time.
  • Request full account deletion.

8. Third-Party Links

NODE may link to external sites (e.g., Binance, BSCScan). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of NODE after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us at support@nodevt.com or through the live chat on our website.
