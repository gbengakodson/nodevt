❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NODE

🤖 What is NODE exactly?
NODE is an Cryptocurrency automation Robo-Advisor. It is designed with a 100grid strategy that flips coins with a buy and sell actions over a given range to generate variable profits as the markets fluctuate. You deposit USDC, activate a Grid Bot, and NODE automatically buys low and sells high within a price range — 24/7. No manual trading. No chart-watching. No experience needed.
⚙️ What is the NODE procedure?
Three steps: 1) Deposit USDC (min $10). 2) Activate a Grid Bot on any coin. The bot places 100 buy/sell orders between a price range. 3) Let it work. When prices move, your bot trades automatically. You collect profit anytime.
🚀 How can I get started?
Register on NODE with your email. You'll get a unique BSC wallet address. Buy USDC from Binance. Send it to your NODE wallet. Once it arrives in your Grand Balance, activate your first Grid Bot. Takes less than 10 minutes.
💵 How do I deposit my first $10?
Buy $10 USDC on Binance. Copy your unique NODE wallet address from your Profile. Withdraw USDC to that address using BSC/BEP20 network. Your Grand Balance updates within 5 minutes automatically.
💸 How do I withdraw my profit?
Click the Grid Profit amount on your Dashboard to collect it to your Yield Wallet. Transfer to Grand Balance when ready. Go to Withdraw page, enter your external wallet address, enter OTP sent to your email. Funds arrive in 1-10 minutes.
🔒 How does NODE secure my capital?
Every user gets their OWN unique BSC wallet. Deposits go to YOUR wallet — verifiable on BSCScan. Withdrawals require OTP verification. Every transaction is on the blockchain and visible on our Transparency page.
