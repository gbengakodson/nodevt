⚙️ How NODE Works

1. Deposit USDC

Fund your personal BSC wallet. As little as $10. You control your keys.

2. Activate a Position Tracker

Pick a coin. The bot places 100 buy/sell orders across a price range.

3. Earn While You Sleep

Grid bots trade 24/7. Profits from market volatility — automatically.

4. Withdraw Anytime

Close your tracker or collect yield. Funds sweep to your wallet.

🔍 Why NODE Was Built

📈 High Barriers

Grid bots require technical knowledge and large capital. Out of reach for most.

🔗 Unified Trading

NODE captures positions and replicates them on real exchange bots.

👥 No Mass Deployment

Lead traders can't deploy bots on thousands of accounts simultaneously.

💰 Pooled Resources

Small deposits combine to activate Master Grid Bots on the Exchanges via BSC.

🛑 Client Interference

Amateur traders disrupt active trades or withdraw funds early.

📊 Transparent Distribution

Real results from central wallets transmitted to NODE. Individual yield based on holdings.
